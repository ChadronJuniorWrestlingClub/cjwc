PRACTICE GUIDE

IMPORTANT NOTES

GETTING THERE

Practice is held in the Chadron High School wrestling room. Enter through the north entrance by the track.

ARRIVAL

Practice begins at 5:15 pm. Have wrestlers ready to start on time. For younger athletes, a parent or guardian waits at the bottom of the stairs until a coach opens the doors and confirms wrestlers may enter.

WHAT TO WEAR

Singlets are not required for practice. Gym shorts and a T-shirt are recommended. Wrestling shoes are helpful, and loaner shoes may be available.

WATER

Bring a water bottle. There is no drinking fountain in the wrestling room.

COMPETITION

TOURNAMENTS

Each wrestler chooses when and where to compete. Families register for the events they select, except for the Chadron Home Tournament, where the club registers and pays for CJWC wrestlers.

Most tournaments charge a registration fee, typically $20-$25. Coaches attend local-area events whenever possible, but confirm that a coach will be available if you are not comfortable helping your wrestler at mat side.

The competition season runs from December through mid-March, finishing with the USA Wrestling Nebraska State Tournament. Wrestlers may continue at additional events outside the regular season. Let the coaches know so the club can continue to support them.

TRAVEL + SUPPORT

If your wrestler wants to compete but your schedule does not allow it, talk to a coach or board member. The club will try to connect families for transportation and mat-side support.

Wyoming and South Dakota events may be AAU-sanctioned. CJWC provides USA Wrestling cards, so families register for AAU separately when an event requires it. Some Nebraska events, including Huskerland State and AAU State, also require an AAU card.

FIND AND SELECT A TOURNAMENT

1. SEARCH

Use TrackWrestling to search events by state and date, or use MapWrestling to filter events by ZIP code, date, distance, age group, and style.

2. CHOOSE

Pick an event that fits your wrestler's age, experience, travel plans, and readiness. Tournaments are optional; there is no pressure to compete before your wrestler is ready.

3. REGISTER

Open the event page and complete the host's registration process. Register and pay for selected events before the posted deadline.

4. CONFIRM

Check with a coach about attendance and tournament-day support, then watch Remind for club updates and changes.
