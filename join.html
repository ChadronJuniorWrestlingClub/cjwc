READY TO WRESTLE?

CWC is a USA Wrestling-sanctioned club. Club membership includes a club-supplied USA Wrestling membership, a team T-shirt and shorts, more than three months of practice, and entry to the Chadron Wrestling Tournament.

To register, fill out the online registration form. It takes about five minutes and works on any phone or computer. Dues are $60 and are due before the first practice. Bring your payment to a coach or board member at parent night or practice.

Prefer paper? Print the registration form and bring it with your payment to parent night, a practice, or a coach or board member.

HOW TO REGISTER

1. Fill out the form. Use the online form, or print the paper form if you prefer.

2. Pay dues. Bring your $60 payment to a coach or board member before the first practice.

3. Paper forms: bring them in person to parent night, a practice, a coach, or a board member. Please do not email paper forms.

Not sure wrestling is right for your child? Every athlete can attend one practice at no cost with a current USA Wrestling card.
