READY TO WRESTLE?

CJWC is a USA Wrestling-sanctioned club. Club membership includes a club-supplied USA Wrestling membership, a team T-shirt and shorts, more than three months of practice, and entry to the Chadron Junior Wrestling Tournament.

To register, fill out the online registration form. It takes about five minutes and works on any phone or computer. Dues are due before the first practice and can be paid by check or Venmo.

Prefer paper? Print the registration form and bring it with your payment to registration night, a practice, or a coach or board member.

Register online →Print registration form →Ask a registration question →

Have a question before registering? Email chadronjrwrestling@gmail.com. If you use the paper form, please bring it and your payment in person rather than emailing it.

REGISTER IN PERSON

Bring your completed registration form and payment to registration night, practice, a coach, or a board member. Do not email the registration form.

Not sure wrestling is right for your child? Every athlete can attend one practice at no cost with a current USA Wrestling card.
