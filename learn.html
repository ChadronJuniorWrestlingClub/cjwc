FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is CWC? +

A youth program teaching wrestling fundamentals, sportsmanship, teamwork, and how to handle individual achievements and disappointments.

Is wrestling dangerous? +

Wrestling is a physical sport and injuries can happen, just as they can in soccer, gymnastics, track, baseball, and football. Rules, trained coaches, and learning safe technique help reduce risk. Youth wrestling is not professional wrestling seen on television.

Does my child need to diet to make weight? +

No. No child is encouraged or recommended to maintain a certain weight.

Can my child try wrestling first? +

Yes. Every athlete can attend one practice at no cost with a current USA Wrestling card.

Where is practice, and what should a wrestler bring? +

Practice is held in the Chadron High School wrestling room. Bring a T-shirt, shorts, wrestling shoes, a water bottle, and warm clothing for winter weather.

What if we cannot afford wrestling shoes? +

Loaner shoes are available when possible. Please return borrowed shoes when your wrestler is done using them.

Do wrestlers have to compete? +

No. Tournaments are optional. The season includes events from January through March, with some pre-season events in December.

What will my child gain from the program? +

Wrestlers build self-discipline, teamwork, persistence, fair play, self-motivation, goal setting, leadership, critical thinking, general health, exercise, and nutrition habits.

Are girls welcome? +

Yes. Girls are welcome and encouraged to participate.
