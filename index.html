05 / GOOD TO KNOW

NEW HERE?

Does my child have to compete? +

No. Tournaments are optional, and each wrestler can start when they and their family feel ready.

Does my child need to cut weight? +

No. The club does not encourage children to maintain a particular weight.

Is wrestling safe? +

Like every physical sport, wrestling carries some risk. It is built around coaching, rules, and safe technique.
